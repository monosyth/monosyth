THE VERMONT DETAILS

Good questions.
Better decisions.

Every property has its own story. These are a few of the practical details worth discussing as you explore your options.

What should I ask about a Vermont home?

Start with the heating system, utility history, water and wastewater systems, road access, maintenance, and any available property disclosures. Ask which details have been documented.

What matters with a multifamily property?

Request current leases, a dated rent roll, operating expenses, unit layouts, and information about repairs and permits. Review access and showing arrangements with Michelle.

How do I prepare to sell?

Begin with your timing and priorities. Gather records of improvements and operating costs, then discuss photography, preparation, showing access, and the information buyers will need.
