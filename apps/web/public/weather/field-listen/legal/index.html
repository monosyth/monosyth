About this field test

This page is a Monosyth-hosted version of the BirdNET Team real-time web app. It uses your device microphone only after you tap Start and grant browser permission. Bird identification runs on your device. Audio is not uploaded to Monosyth or added to the weather station's bird history.

Bird images can load from Cornell's BirdNET service. Standard website access information may be handled by the hosting provider. Location access is off by default; you can enable it in Settings to filter likely species around you.
